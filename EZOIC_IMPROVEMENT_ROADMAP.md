## Phase 2: Content Depth & Quality

### Expand Existing Guides

- **Beginner's Guide:** Add a short "Austin-Specific Tips" section (best courts for new players, local clinics, etc.)
- **Choosing Paddle:** Add "Austin Player Favorites" or tie-in to local play conditions (heat, humidity)
- **Rules:** Add a "Common Questions" or mini-FAQ section
- **Improving Game:** Add "Practice Drills You Can Do Solo" or "Where to Practice in Austin"

### Court Pages

- Consider adding 1–2 short paragraphs of **editorial content** to key court pages (e.g., "Why players love this court," "Best for beginners," "Great for tournaments")
- Ensures court pages read less like pure directory listings and more like valuable editorial

## Phase 3: UX & Trust Enhancements

### Sidebars on Guide Pages

Ezoic recommends: *"Include a Sidebar widget with 'About' and 'Recommended Articles.'"*

- Add a **sidebar** (or mobile-friendly equivalent) to guide pages with:
  - **About Pickleball ATX** (short blurb + link to /about)
  - **Recommended Guides** (3–4 related guide links)

### Homepage

- Add a **"Latest Guides"** or **"Popular Guides"** section if not present
- Ensure homepage clearly showcases both directory + editorial value

### Internal Linking

- Add **"Related Guides"** at the bottom of each guide
- Link guides to relevant court pages and vice versa

## Phase 4: Unique Differentiators

### Local Authority

- **Testimonials or quotes** from Austin players (even 2–3 can help)
- **"Community Picks"** or **"Player Favorites"** callout on court pages
- Consider a simple **"Submit a Review"** or feedback mechanism (even if lightweight)

### Fresh Content Signals

- Add **"Last updated"** dates to court pages where feasible
- Consider a **"What's New"** or **"Recent Updates"** section on homepage or in footer
